      Assumption sensitivity

      The same seed with volatility halved and increased by half.

      This is not the probability that a stock rises.

      The model assumes constant drift, constant volatility and normally distributed shocks. Real markets have jumps, regime changes, costs and liquidity constraints. Until parameters are calibrated against valid historical data, these numbers are illustrative scenarios only.

      Method, limits & design

      No hidden logic. Everything below describes exactly what the tool does and what it deliberately does not do.

      The formula

      A weighted average of six 0–100 inputs.

      S = Σ ( wi × xi )  ·  xi ∈ [0, 100]

      Weights are normalised to sum to 1 before scoring. If any factor is missing, the row is marked incomplete and no score is produced — missing evidence is never imputed and never treated as zero.
